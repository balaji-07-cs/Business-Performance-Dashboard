Business Performance Dashboard

📊 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of my **30-Day Data Analytics Internship**.

The objective was to build an interactive **Business Performance Dashboard** using Power BI to analyze key business KPIs, sales performance, customer behavior, and business trends.

The dashboard converts business data into interactive visual insights that can support management decision-making.


 🎯 Business Objectives

* Monitor overall business performance
* Analyze revenue and sales trends
* Understand customer and transaction behavior
* Identify important business KPIs
* Compare performance across categories and locations
* Identify potential business risks and opportunities
* Support data-driven decision-making


 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI Desktop**
* **Power Query**
* **DAX**
* **Microsoft Excel**
* **Python / Pandas** for data analysis and preprocessing
* **GitHub** for project documentation and portfolio management

---

⚠️ Business Risk Areas

The analysis highlighted areas that should be monitored:

* Missing product information
* Missing price and quantity values
* Missing discount information
* Data consistency issues
* Potential impact of data quality on reporting accuracy

Improving data quality can make future dashboards and business decisions more reliable.

---

 Strategic Recommendations

1. Improve Data Quality

Implement validation rules and standardize transaction fields before dashboard refreshes.

2. Monitor Sales Performance

Track revenue, AOV, transaction volume, category performance, and location performance regularly.

3. Improve Customer & Promotion Strategy

Analyze customer purchasing behavior and discount effectiveness before implementing future promotional campaigns.
